C7.2.2 Initialize snapshot rows for existing accounts

Requirement: Every account must have a snapshot row.
Expected result: No null lookups; balance fetch always works.
Approach: Insert missing rows.

INSERT INTO account_balance_snapshots (account_id, balance_minor)
SELECT a.account_id, 0
FROM accounts a
LEFT JOIN account_balance_snapshots s ON s.account_id = a.account_id
WHERE s.account_id IS NULL;
