C7.2.4 Change “available credit check” to use snapshot



Requirement: No more SUM(...) on every purchase.
Expected result: Purchase endpoint stays fast even at high ledger volume.
Approach:
	•	SELECT balance_minor FROM account_balance_snapshots FOR UPDATE
	•	compute available = credit_limit_minor + balance_minor
	•	approve/decline
	•	insert ledger rows
	•	update snapshots


	C7.2.4 Micro tasks

Requirement
	•	Balance/available credit must not compute from full ledger in hot path.
	•	Snapshot must be locked (FOR UPDATE) to prevent race approvals.
	•	If snapshot missing, fallback to rebuild (fail-safe) OR decline.

Expected Result
	•	Purchase stays fast with huge ledger.
	•	Concurrent purchases cannot both approve using stale balance.

Approach
	•	In purchase flow:
	•	lock account row (FOR UPDATE)
	•	ensure snapshot exists and lock it
	•	compute available credit from snapshot
	•	post two ledger legs
	•	update snapshot
	•	commit
